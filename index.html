Case study

My portfolio website

I'm a beginner web designer, and I designed my own portfolio in Figma to practice layout, color and prototyping.

Client
Personal project
Role
Web designer
Timeline
2 weeks
Tools
Figma

What was broken?

As a beginner, I had no portfolio and no projects to show yet, so I had no way to share my skills.

What did you do?

As a beginner, I designed my own portfolio in Figma. I made five pages, chose a purple, gold and black style, and linked them into a clickable prototype.

What changed?

I now have a portfolio design with a clickable prototype, and I learned layout, color and prototyping.

Skills

Process

  1. PlanI decided what pages and content my portfolio needs.
  2. StyleI chose the colors, fonts and layout.
  3. DesignI built the pages in Figma.
  4. PrototypeI linked the pages so they work like a real site.

Results

  • 5 pages designed
  • 1 clickable prototype
  • 1 screen size (desktop)
